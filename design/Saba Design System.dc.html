02 — FOUNDATIONS
Dark mode: keeping the dark elements readable
The identity is dark-on-light, so a naive dark theme erases it. The rule is: dark elements do not stay dark — they keep their role by inverting or by rising. Three different fixes, one per element class.
1 · Primary buttons invert
Ink and paper swap. color/primary becomes near-white and color/on-primary becomes near-black, so the primary action is still the highest-contrast thing on screen.
Buy Now
Buy Now
2 · The header card rises
It was dark to separate itself from a white page. In dark mode it becomes the lightest surface on the page — #1F2730 plus a hairline border. Same shape, same role, opposite direction.
3 · The nav keeps its white pill
The bar lifts to #1F2730 with a border and a stronger shadow so it still reads as floating; the active pill stays white. The signature is the pill, not the bar.
Home
Home
!
The one thing never to do: keep a dark fill on a dark page. If a component was dark in light mode, in dark mode it is either inverted (buttons, active pill, corner action button, rating pill) or elevated with a border (header card, nav bar, bottom sheet, dialog). There is no third option, and no component keeps #14181D in dark mode.
03 — FOUNDATIONS
Type scale
One family, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, for Arabic and Latin. It has real weights at 300–700, sharp Latin, and Arabic that keeps its structure at small sizes. Arabic runs longer and taller: every level gets +0.12 line height and drops the negative tracking, which is a Latin-only optical fix.

The weight gap is the hierarchy
Headings are 700, body is 400. Nothing sits at 500 except numerals in dense merchant tiles. Two weights, far apart, is what stops the app reading flat — 600 is reserved for button and chip labels only.
Numerals stay Western in both languages
Iraqi users read 250,000 far faster than ٢٥٠٬٠٠٠ in a price. Digits are Western with a comma group separator; only the currency mark localises: د.ع / IQD. Always tabular so columns of prices align.

How separation works
In order of preference: space first — 32 between sections does the job of a divider. Then a soft neutral fill (surface/sunken) behind product images and inactive chips. Then a 1px border where a card must hold its own edge against a same-colour background. Shadow is used on exactly three things: the floating nav, the sticky buy bar and the bottom sheet — elevation/float: 0 8px 24px rgba(14,26,43,.10). Divider lines appear only inside a totals table, where rows are genuinely a table.

Inputs and selection
Fields are filled, not outlined, at rest — surface/sunken with no border. The border only appears on focus and on error, so an errored field is visible from across the screen. Errors sit below the field in words, and the field keeps what the user typed.

09 — WRITING
Short bold title, one plain sentence
Every choice, setting, option and empty state is written the same way: a title of two to four words, then one sentence saying what happens when the user taps it. No marketing, no jargon, no second sentence. The Arabic is written natively, not translated word for word.
I'm a customer
Browse stores and buy safely with escrow.
I'm a merchant
Open a store, list products and reach buyers near you.

Write this
“Only 3 left” · “Your store is under review. We check documents within two working days.” · “Saved for later. It moves back to your cart when you tap it.”
