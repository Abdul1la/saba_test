02 — FOUNDATIONS
Dark mode: keeping the dark elements readable
The identity is dark-on-light, so a naive dark theme erases it. The rule is:
dark elements do not stay dark — they keep their role by inverting or by rising.
Three different fixes, one per element class.
1 · Primary buttons invert
Ink and paper swap.
color/primary
becomes near-white and
color/on-primary
becomes near-black, so the primary action is still the highest-contrast thing on screen.
Buy Now
Buy Now
2 · The header card rises
It was dark to separate itself from a white page. In dark mode it becomes the
lightest
surface on the page —
#1F2730
plus a hairline border. Same shape, same role, opposite direction.
3 · The nav keeps its white pill
The bar lifts to
#1F2730
with a border and a stronger shadow so it still reads as floating; the active pill stays white. The signature is the pill, not the bar.
Home
Home
!
The one thing never to do:
keep a dark fill on a dark page. If a component was dark in light mode, in dark mode it is either inverted (buttons, active pill, corner action button, rating pill) or elevated with a border (header card, nav bar, bottom sheet, dialog). There is no third option, and no component keeps
#14181D
in dark mode.

The weight gap is the hierarchy
Headings are 700, body is 400. Nothing sits at 500 except numerals in dense merchant tiles. Two weights, far apart, is what stops the app reading flat — 600 is reserved for button and chip labels only.
Numerals stay Western in both languages
Iraqi users read 250,000 far faster than ٢٥٠٬٠٠٠ in a price. Digits are Western with a comma group separator; only the currency mark localises:
د.ع
/ IQD. Always tabular so columns of prices align.

09 — WRITING
Short bold title, one plain sentence
Every choice, setting, option and empty state is written the same way: a title of two to four words, then one sentence saying what happens when the user taps it. No marketing, no jargon, no second sentence. The Arabic is written natively, not translated word for word.
I'm a customer
Browse stores and buy safely with escrow.
I'm a merchant
Open a store, list products and reach buyers near you.
أنا عميل
تصفّح المتاجر واشترِ بأمان عبر الضمان.
أنا تاجر
افتح متجراً واعرض منتجاتك ووصّلها لمشترين قريبين منك.
Write this
“Only 3 left” · “Your store is under review. We check documents within two working days.” · “Saved for later. It moves back to your cart when you tap it.”
Not this
“Hurry — almost gone!” · “Verification pending.” · “Item successfully moved to the saved items list.”
